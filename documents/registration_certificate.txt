REGISTRATION CERTIFICATE IN GERMANY (MELDEBESCHEINIGUNG / ANMELDEBESTÄTIGUNG)
Source: iamexpat.de (updated April 2025)

WHAT IS THE REGISTRATION CERTIFICATE?
The registration certificate is a document that proves you are officially registered at an address in Germany. There are two versions:

1. ANMELDEBESTÄTIGUNG (Registration confirmation):
- The document you receive immediately when you first register (Anmeldung) at the citizens' office.
- Shows your original registration date (when you first moved into the property).
- Given to you once, at your registration appointment.

2. MELDEBESCHEINIGUNG (Copy of registration record):
- A copy/extract of your registration record.
- Shows the date the copy was made and your current registered address.
- Required when authorities need proof that is less than 6 months old.
- The one you will need most often for administrative tasks.

WHAT INFORMATION IS ON THE REGISTRATION CERTIFICATE?
- Registration date
- Move-in date at current address
- Your full address in Germany
- Your full name and date of birth
- Official stamp and date from the registration authority (Meldebehörde)

WHAT DO YOU NEED THE REGISTRATION CERTIFICATE FOR?
You need it for almost every official process in Germany:
- Applying for a residence permit (Aufenthaltstitel) at the Foreigners' Office.
- Opening a German bank account.
- Getting health insurance.
- Enrolling at a university or school.
- Getting a mobile phone contract.
- Starting a new job (employer often asks for it).
- Applying for Bürgergeld or unemployment benefits at the Jobcenter.
- Getting a Tax ID.
- Applying for permanent residence (Niederlassungserlaubnis).

HOW TO GET A MELDEBESCHEINIGUNG:
Option 1 — ONLINE (in some cities):
- Berlin: eww.berlin.de/buergerdienste/app/meldebescheinigung
- Frankfurt: frankfurt.de/leistungen (search Meldebescheinigung)
- Munich: stadt.muenchen.de (search Meldebescheinigung)
- Stuttgart: www.stuttgart.de (search Meldebescheinigung)
- Check verwaltung.bund.de to see if your area offers online service.
- Note: online option requires an activated eID card.

Option 2 — IN PERSON at your local Bürgeramt:
- Book an appointment (select "Meldebescheinigung beantragen" as the reason).
- Bring: valid passport or ID card.
- Small fee: €5 to €15 (varies by city).
- You receive the certificate on the same day.

EXTENDED REGISTRATION CERTIFICATE (ERWEITERTE MELDEBESCHEINIGUNG):
Sometimes required (rarely). Contains additional information:
- Your previous addresses in Germany.
- Your registered spouse's details.
- Your children's details.
Request it in person at the citizens' office. Bring ID and pay a small fee.

IMPORTANT NOTE FOR RESIDENCE PERMIT APPLICATIONS:
Most authorities require a Meldebescheinigung that is no older than 6 months. The Anmeldebestätigung you received years ago is NOT sufficient — get a fresh Meldebescheinigung.

HOW TO GET YOUR ANMELDEBESTÄTIGUNG (First Registration):
You receive this automatically when you register for the first time at the Bürgeramt. See the Anmeldung document for the full registration process.

WHAT HAPPENS IF YOU LOSE YOUR REGISTRATION CERTIFICATE?
You can get a replacement Meldebescheinigung at any time from the citizens' office in person, or online in supported cities. A small fee applies.
